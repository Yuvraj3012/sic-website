About

A club run like a small fund, taught like a seminar.

SIC brings together USF students from every major who want to understand markets by working in them — pitching stocks, managing real capital, and learning directly from people who do this for a living.

Ideas

Weekly pitch meetings where members present, defend, and challenge investment theses in front of their peers.

People

A community of analysts, alumni, faculty advisors, and industry guests across San Francisco's financial district.

Opportunities

Recruiting prep, mentorship, and a direct line to internships in banking, asset management, and trading.

Student Managed Investment Fund

Real capital. Real decisions.

The SMIF is a long-only equity portfolio managed entirely by students. Sector teams source ideas, build models, and bring recommendations to an investment committee vote.

6Sector coverage teams
25Target portfolio positions
WeeklyInvestment committee
S&P 500Performance benchmark

  1. Screen

    Sector teams screen their universe and surface names worth a closer look.

  2. Model

    Analysts build a three-statement model, a DCF, and a comps set.

  3. Pitch

    The team presents the thesis, catalysts, and risks to the committee.

  4. Monitor

    Approved positions are tracked against price targets each quarter.

Education

From first trade to final round.

A four-stage curriculum taught by upperclassmen and alumni. No finance background needed — new members start at the beginning.

Stage 1

Markets 101

How stocks, bonds, and the economy fit together.

Stage 2

Accounting & Valuation

Reading financial statements, DCF, and comparable companies.

Stage 3

Modeling

Building three-statement and LBO models in Excel.

Stage 4

Interview Prep

Technicals, behaviorals, and mock superdays.
